\section{Weights}
\label{sec:weights}
The last part of the elicitation is the definition of the weights applying the \gls{bwt} methodology presented in section \ref{sec:bwt}. This session was deemed intesive from the experts due to the intrinsic difficulty of the questions at hand. This must not be judged as a negative aspect, since it reflects the difficulty of a tradeoff problem. Nonetheless this has to considered by the analyist when planning elicitation sessions, to avoid tireness and loss of focus that could compromise the quality of the answers. For the comaprison another graphical tool was developed in python, some screenshots of the interface are presented in Figure \ref{fig:weights_elicitation}. Unfortunatly in this case it is not possible to circumvent the time consuming nature of this task due to the method itself which requires the expert to reason about tradeoffs. Some results from different experts are presented in Figure \ref{fig:weights_examples} to show the variability in preferences that can be observed. To ensure consistency, since the \gls{bwt} method requires the knowledge of value functions, only experts that already participated in the value function elicitation sessions were involved in this step.
