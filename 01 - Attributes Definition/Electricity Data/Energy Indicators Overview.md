## Electricity Loads and Flows
Data has been obtained from the [ENTSOE transparency platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/). 
The zone codes (EIC code) where obtained from [the official list](https://www.entsoe.eu/data/energy-identification-codes-eic/eic-approved-codes/).
Carbon intensity data from [Electricity Maps](https://www.electricitymaps.com/), data sources are ENTSOE for EU countries and *EMBER + Electricity Maps Estimation* for CH.

To verify **EIC** codes [here](https://transparencyplatform.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15885757676308-Area-List-with-Energy-Identification-Code-EIC).

**EIC** Zones analysed:
- Italy
- CH
- Poland
- Germany (In doubt if this is relevant or not)

Data obtained:
- Load
- Cross-Border Flows


### Ways to use this data
- Energy-wise indicator vs Time-wise indicator: Poland has a problem of unstable power generation, it doesn't import that much energy but it has to do it very frequently, therefore load following capabilities are more important for this country
- Average Import: represent how self-sufficient is the country's grid, to be also compared with the net cross-border flow to better understand if the need is physical or rather a matter of where the electricity is generated and therefore grid transmission limitations (see difference between Italy and Germany)
